About us

Practical research for people who make videos.

Creator Tool Bench is an independent editorial project about AI tools used in video creation.

What we cover

We focus on practical creator tasks: generating subtitles, transcribing audio, editing videos and turning long-form content into short clips. We aim to explain workflows and trade-offs in plain English.

How we work

We distinguish product information verified from official sources from observations made during hands-on testing. When a tool has not been tested by us, we say so. We do not invent user reviews, benchmark results, prices, product features or testimonials.

Independence

Some pages may contain affiliate links. A commission may be earned if a reader purchases through those links, at no additional cost to the reader. Commercial relationships do not change our obligation to describe limitations accurately. See our Affiliate Disclosure.
